# HiDef Radio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

HiDef Radio is an Android app published by derek20la ("the developer"). It receives over-the-air radio with an RTL-SDR USB dongle.

## The short version

HiDef Radio does not collect, send or share any personal data. It could not if it tried: the app has no internet permission.

## What the app does with data

- **Radio reception.** The signal from your USB dongle is decoded on your phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- **Data kept on your phone.** Your favorites, the names you give to stations and programs, your settings, the last station you tuned, station logos received over the air, and the tuner gain that worked for each station. This data stays in the app's private storage on your device.
- **Removing it.** Settings > Clear saved data removes logos and remembered gains. Clearing the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstalling the app, removes everything.
- **Android backup.** If you have switched on Android's own backup, Android may include this app's settings and favorites in the backup of your device in your own Google account. The developer has no access to it.

## What the app does not do

- No account, no sign-in.
- No ads.
- No analytics, no tracking, no third-party data-collecting software.
- No access to your location, contacts, microphone, camera or files.

## Permissions

- **USB device** - to talk to the radio dongle you plug in. Android asks you each time, unless you tell it to remember.
- **Notifications** - for the "now playing" notification with its play / pause buttons.
- **Foreground service (media playback)** and **wake lock** - so the radio keeps playing with the screen off.

## Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google may give the developer anonymous, aggregated statistics such as install counts and crash reports. That is governed by Google's privacy policy; the app itself sends nothing.

## Data retention and deletion

The developer receives and holds no user data, so there is nothing to retain or delete. Everything the app saves is on your device and under your control (see "Removing it").

## Children

HiDef Radio is a general-audience app and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
